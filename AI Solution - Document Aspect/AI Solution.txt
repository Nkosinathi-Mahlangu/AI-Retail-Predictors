-  AI Solution: Relevance to Theme (Background)  
Fuel filling stations across South Africa increasingly operate hybrid retail environments — combining 
a forecourt with a convenience store and, in many cases, an in-house food kitchen — yet most still rely on
manual stock counts and manager intuition to decide what to order and when. 
This directly reflects the Theme, “an AI Solution for Industries”: while 4IR technologies are widely discussed,
small and medium retail operators such as filling stations remain largely excluded from their benefits. 
This project proposes an AI-powered Sales and Demand Prediction System, built in Python, that treats the store
and the kitchen as one integrated system with two linked data streams. The system uses historical sales 
data (product sold, quantity, price, day of week, promotions, weather conditions and current stock levels) to 
forecast future demand for both convenience-store products and kitchen ingredients, alerting managers when predicted
demand is likely to exceed available stock so that ordering decisions become proactive rather than reactive.
