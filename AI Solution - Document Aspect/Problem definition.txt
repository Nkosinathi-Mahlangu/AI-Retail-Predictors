Filling station convenience stores and the kitchens inside these stores currently order stock
based on manual estimation and staff experience rather than data-driven forecasting.
This causes two common problems: stockouts, where fast-moving items such as
bottled water, snacks and key kitchen ingredients run out during high-demand periods
such as weekends, holidays or promotions, resulting in lost sales and frustrated
customers and overstocking, where slow-moving or perishable items particularly
fresh kitchen ingredients are ordered in excess and go to waste before they can be
sold. Demand fluctuates with variables such as day of the week, weather conditions
and promotional activity, making it difficult for staff to predict it accurately across
multiple product categories in the store and kitchen at the same time. As a result
stores experience inconsistent stock availability, avoidable financial losses from waste,
lost sales and an inability to make timely, informed purchasing decisions, which
undermines profitability and customer service.
This problem is directly relevant to the theme, “an AI Solution for Industries” because
it applies 4IR technology to a small/medium retail industry currently excluded from
most AI adoption. Solving it benefits the business through improved profitability and
reduced waste and benefits the surrounding community where the
station serves a small town through more reliable access to essential convenience
goods and food and continued local employment.
