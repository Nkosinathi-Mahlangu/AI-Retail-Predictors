**Questions to Ask or Test the AI**
This document contains example questions for testing the SmartFuel AI assistant. Ask each question in the AI Assistant dashboard and verify responses against the available sales data, forecasts, and inventory calculations.

**1. Demand & Forecasting**
- What is the predicted demand for P0005?
- What is the predicted demand for P0010?
- Which products have the highest predicted demand?
- Which products have the lowest predicted demand?
- What products are expected to have high demand tomorrow?
- What products are expected to have low demand?
- What is the expected demand for this week?
- Which products are showing increasing demand?
- Which products are showing decreasing demand?
- What are the top 10 products by predicted demand?
- Which categories have the highest predicted demand?
- What is the forecast for a specific product category?
  
**2. Inventory & Stockouts**
- What products are likely to run out?
- Which products are at high stockout risk?
- Which products are at medium stockout risk?
- Which products have enough stock?
- Which products have the lowest inventory levels?
- What products are below their expected demand?
- Which products need immediate attention?
- Are there any products that may run out soon?
- Which products have more inventory than expected demand?
- Which products are overstocked?
  
**3. Reordering**
- Which products should I reorder?
- What should I order today?
- How many units of P0005 should I order?
- What is the recommended order quantity for P0010?
- Which products need urgent reordering?
- Show me the top 10 reorder priorities.
- Which products require the largest orders?
- What products should I reorder first?
- Are there products that don't need to be reordered?
- How much stock should I order to meet predicted demand?
  
**4. Categories & Business Performance**
- Which category has the highest demand?
- Which category has the lowest demand?
- What are the top 5 highest-demand categories?
- Which categories have the most products at risk?
- Which category needs the most stock?
- Which category has the highest inventory level?
- Which categories should I focus on today?
- What products are driving demand in the highest-demand category?
  
**5. Store & Region Analysis**
- Which store has the highest predicted demand?
- Which store has the highest stockout risk?
- Which store needs the most reordering?
- Which region has the highest demand?
- Which region has the lowest demand?
- What products are most popular in this region?
- Which stores have low inventory?
- Compare demand between stores.
- Which store has the most products requiring attention?
  
**6. Promotions & Conditions**
- How does promotion affect predicted demand?
- Which products have high demand during promotions?
- What is the demand for products currently on promotion?
- Which products should we stock more of during promotions?
- How does weather affect demand?
- Which products have high demand under the current weather condition?
- Which products are affected by seasonality?
  
**7. Top 10 Questions for a Presentation**
1. Which products should I reorder?
2. What products are likely to run out?
3. What is the predicted demand for P0005?
4. How many units of P0005 should I order?
5. Show me the top 10 products by predicted demand.
6. Which products have high stockout risk?
7. Which categories have the highest demand?
8. Which products need immediate attention?
9. Which store has the highest predicted demand?
10. What products should I stock more of during promotions?
    
**Testing Guidelines**
- Enter questions one at a time in the AI Assistant dashboard.
- Check numerical answers against the forecast, inventory, and processed sales data.
- Verify that product IDs, categories, stores, and regions match the available dataset.
- Check reorder quantities and stockout risks against the system's inventory calculations.
- Record answers that are incorrect, incomplete, or unsupported so they can be investigated.
- Do not assume an answer is correct simply because it sounds plausible; verify it against the underlying data.
