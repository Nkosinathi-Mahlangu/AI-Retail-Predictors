AI Retail Predictors — Business Objectives
==========================================

Problem Statement
-----------------
Filling-station forecourt managers currently rely on manual
judgement to make daily stock-ordering decisions. This leads
to overstocking, understocking, and missed sales opportunities.

Business Objectives
-------------------
1. Reduce stockout incidents by providing next-day demand
   forecasts per product and store.
2. Flag products at risk of running out before the next
   delivery so managers can prioritise restock orders.
3. Provide a natural-language chatbot interface so that
   non-technical managers can query forecasts without reading
   spreadsheets or understanding data science.

Target User
-----------
Non-technical filling-station forecourt manager.

Dataset
-------
Kaggle "Retail Store Inventory and Demand Forecasting"
(synthetic data — see Data Privacy section in README.md).
